# Let'sMOD (Polyup)

> Let'sMOD (formerly Polyup) is a Sunnyvale, California consumer platform where users aged 8+ play, modify, remix, and share short 3D microgames and AI-powered interactive memes. A 500 Global portfolio company. No public developer API, SDK, or developer portal is published today.
